Research Mission & Philosophical Thesis

This platform represents an ongoing, living research initiative spearheaded by Shunchao Zhou. The fundamental mission is to bridge the chasm between probabilistic artificial intelligence and the zero-tolerance determinism demanded by corporate accounting and institutional capital markets.

Core Pillar 01
Beyond Static Rules & Brittle RPA
Traditional financial software relies on fragile regex and rigid scripts. We research how cognitive AI Agents perceive chaotic, unstructured documents and handle long-tail exceptions autonomously.
Core Pillar 02
Multi-Agent Dialectics & Debate
Financial decision-making requires opposing viewpoints. By pitting specialized Bull vs Bear or Auditor vs Bookkeeper agents against each other, we eliminate hallucination and confirmation bias.
Core Pillar 03
Verifiable Mathematical Grounding
AI must never invent numbers. We enforce strict deterministic code guardrails where every double-entry balance, tax computation, and DCF multiple is verified with code and citation tracking.
